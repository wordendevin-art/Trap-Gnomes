# Trap Gnomes V1 - changelog (newest first)
# Shown in the menu's Changelog tab. A copy is built into the mod, and the menu also reads
# this file from GitHub, so edits here reach everyone without a new download.
#
# "## <version>" starts a release. Lines under it begin with:
#   +  added      *  changed or fixed      -  removed
# Lines starting with "#" are notes like these and are never shown.
# Start a line with [Customer], [Admin] or [Owner] (after the + * -) to show it only
# to that role and up. Untagged lines are shown to everyone. The tag is never displayed.

## 1.4.1
+ Automatic updates: new versions download and install themselves and start at your next launch (turn it off in the Info tab)
+ ADMIN tag: players with the Admin role get their own blue ADMIN tag above their gnome, seen by every mod user
* [Admin] No Clip: you can no longer get ragdolled or dragged under the map while flying through walls
* Hulk Mode and Gnome Size: your hands grab and hold things again at any size
* [Customer] Hulk Mode: punches, boxing gloves, bats, pickaxes and branches now knock out the human and other creatures in one hit
* [Admin] Host > Debug: tasks show their real names, and completing one counts it properly (deposit and end-of-day list)
* [Admin] The end-of-day task list no longer comes up empty
* Big FPS boost: the mod no longer repeats expensive game lookups every frame

## 1.4.0
+ The Free Version now works without a key - a license key unlocks Customer or Admin (Info tab > Enter license key)
+ Gnome Customization: recolour your gnome with a live preview you can spin - everyone gets palette colours and 3 presets
+ [Customer] Customer and up: fine-tune sliders, Solid / Rainbow / Glow, and your outfit split into Hat, Robe, Sleeves and Belt and Boots to colour one by one, plus 9 Presets
+ Items tab: colour each piece of equipment on its own, with the item shown in the preview
+ Gnome Size slider (50-200%) - your camera follows your gnome's height in first and third person - your hitbox grows and shrinks with it
+ [Customer] Hulk Mode (Mods section, Customer and up): a huge, muscular, green gnome with God Mode, extra speed, super strength (right-click push) and a hold-Jump Hulk leap
+ Customization saves as you go - no Save button (Hulk Mode starts off each launch)
+ Players running the mod see each other's gnome looks, size, Hulk Mode and Middle Finger, live
+ [Admin] Spectator Mode (Player tab): watch your teammates with a free-look third-person camera (or through their eyes), with their name, health and what they carry on screen - your gnome hides from everyone while you watch
+ Keybinds for the menu and every mod your role can use (Menu Settings > Keybinds)
+ [Customer] ESP tab with NPC and Item ESP, plus Box and Bones options
+ [Admin] Player ESP and the new Player Inventory ESP
+ [Customer] Skeleton ESP on non-human enemies like the Cat
+ Modders Detected list in the top-right corner, including item-spawn detection
+ Changelog tab, Report a Bug button, and a warning when you're on an old version
+ [Customer] Network stats in the Info tab and the On-Screen Info panel: ping, packet delivery, game traffic and connection speed
+ Your license key is remembered after you enter it once
* The mouse no longer turns your camera while the menu is open - no need to pause
* Keys and clicks no longer move your gnome or pause the game while the menu is open (typing in it still works)
* Fixed the gnome shaking in the customizer preview
* Gnome Customization no longer switches you to Third Person
* Other mod users' looks now show up even if they join after you closed the menu
* Players with Japanese, Chinese or Korean names no longer slow the game down
* Smoother menu: fades in and out, and a popup shows when a hotkey toggles a mod
* [Customer] Item ESP now only shows items in the world
* [Customer] Fixed the No Ragdoll patch not loading
* Fixed the main menu background disappearing when Third Person was left on
* Performance Mode now also turns off post-processing in-game, for more FPS
* Automatic cache cleanup and a 16 MB log cap for steadier FPS
* General performance improvements
